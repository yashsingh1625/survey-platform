THE RESEARCH

Your commute is different. We want to understand why.

People travel similar routes every day, but their experiences can be very different. Some spend too much, some lose time, some struggle with reliability, and others simply prefer travelling alone.

Instead of assuming what the problem is, we want to hear from people who actually commute.

TRUST & SAFETY INQUIRY

What would make shared commuting comfortable?

Trust may be one of the biggest factors in shared commuting. We want to understand what people actually need.

We are researching the factors below as exploratory possibilities — not active service promises.

1 Community verification
2 Identity verification
3 Ratings & reviews
4 Previous ride history
5 Mutual connections
6 Live location sharing
7 Emergency / SOS
8 Clear profile information
9 Knowing the person beforehand
10 Repeat commute connections (familiar everyday faces)
Which of these actually matter to you? Tell us in the survey.
Help us understand what would make you feel secure.
